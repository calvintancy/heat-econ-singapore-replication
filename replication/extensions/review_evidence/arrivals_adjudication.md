It re-estimates the covariance for every draw and independently applies the declared rank/HC0 availability rules. There are no lagged-outcome regressors, permutations, native refits or estimated empirical nulls.

The files bind the exact X/Y/calendars/names and native arrays to the immutable baseline. These are genuine null rejection rates **under the stated conditional fixed-design DGP**, not estimates of the actual arrivals' error law. They justify withholding headline interpretation from these nominal multi-DF p-values; they do not establish a universal cutoff at eight restrictions or invalidate every scalar test.

**The static origin-cluster common-shock limitation is algebraic.** On the balanced country/YoY/long/event-excluded own-current common-slope design, a deterministic common-date perturbation changes the slope by +18.4631 while changing origin-CR1 covariance by only 1.05e−15 relatively. This is a sensitivity identity, not an empirical-null experiment; its exact static-balanced result is not asserted for dynamic or PPML models. **Origin-year robustness changes profiles, not their equally weighted mean.** Across all 24 matched requests, average total-temperature slopes differ from their parents by at most 4.88e−13, and shared controls by 6.75e−13. A separate small mean/deviation regression confirms the decomposition. There are 85 sign changes among 612 individual slopes, with changes summing to zero. This follows from balanced panels and common stochastic regressors: the added origin-year nuisance space changes origin deviations while leaving the common mean equation intact. These fits are useful profile sensitivity checks, not independent confirmation of an official national aggregate effect.

**Calendar arithmetic is correct; finite-sample convention matters.** The selected gapped two-channel model reproduces the full calendar-HAC matrices and alternative scaling calculations. Missing months remain zero-score calendar bins. The comparison CSV labels all alternatives as conventions, not uniquely correct reference laws.

## Qualified or not established

- The review's blanket statements that *no* joint p-value can be evidence, or that failure begins at q≈8, go beyond the selected counterexamples. Retain the demonstrated failure and the absence of a general calibration claim; do not substitute a universal q/time-df rule, Hotelling law or alternative critical value.
- Its small pointwise rejection shares are descriptive counts of dependent, heterogeneous tests. They establish neither an all-null model nor absence of an effect. Holm within one origin profile does not control the whole research grid.
- Its iid/MA(11) scalar reference laws are explicitly conditional benchmarks. Treating observed AR columns as fixed in the thin dynamic examples is not a coherent simulation of every underlying dynamic-growth process. No empirical calibration of the observed regressions follows.
- The review snapshot intentionally omitted 84 optional monthly-preflight files and one earlier report-verification file. These are the 85 unavailable pins, not missing actual estimation samples. The present pins matched; current parent samples and stochastic bases were independently reconstructed. This remains an explicit snapshot-coverage limitation.
- Producer manifests retain their computation-time state; separately bound independent verification receipts are authoritative. Annual Lunar New Year days are omitted because their annual total is constant and collinear with intercepts. Neither bookkeeping point is a numerical error.
- Seventeen annual growth years, unavailable full-control annual fits, incomplete growth stationarity screens, common-weather identification, small time df and exploratory multiplicity remain material limits. No Stata/browser execution was performed by this Fable lane.

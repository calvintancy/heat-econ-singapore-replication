__TABLE_PANELS__

Baseline: rainfall, humidity, wind and PM2.5. The full-control column is shown first. The longer history without wind/PM2.5 is robustness; COVID exclusion is also robustness.

All models include sector-specific seasonality and year fixed effects. For IIP, the broad panel has six manufacturing clusters; the detailed panel has 18 industries. VA uses 11 broad or 19 detailed sectors. Matched-sample columns use identical dates within each outcome and frequency.

MoM = month-on-month; QoQ = quarter-on-quarter. The outcome is 100 × one-period change in log seasonally adjusted output, unless labelled NSA; growth is not annualised. Monthly and quarterly coefficients are different estimands. IIP measures real manufacturing production output; VA measures real value added. These remain conditional associations rather than identified causal heat effects.

Common-slope panel tables cluster by sector (unit i), using CR1 and t(S−1). The DJO label identifies the log-growth transformation, with frequency adapted; it does not imply the full annual multicountry design. For QoQ/MoM growth, β=−1 means about 1 percentage point less period growth per +1°C, conditional on controls and FE. This is not by itself evidence of a permanent growth loss.
